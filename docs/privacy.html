clip'M

Privacy Policy

14 October 2026

clip'M is a clipboard manager for Mac, developed by United Visions. What you copy stays on your Mac. We do not run an account system, and we do not collect your clipboard.

What stays on your Mac

clip'M saves clipboard history, snippets, and action scripts in ~/Library/Application Support/ClipM on that computer. History can include text, links, file names, and images you copied. The App Store version keeps this folder inside its macOS app container. Preferences, such as keyboard shortcuts, stay on the Mac as well.

The contents of saved clips and snippets are encrypted on this Mac before they are written down. That covers copied text, rich text, PDFs, file names, links, and images, plus snippet titles and text. Encryption is AES-GCM. Each Mac gets its own random keys, kept in the Keychain, available while that Mac is unlocked, and not synced to other devices. History and snippets use different keys. The times a clip was saved or last used are stored so the list can be ordered, and those times are not encrypted. Preferences and action scripts are stored on the Mac and are not encrypted. The clipboard macOS is holding right now is the system clipboard; clip'M encrypts the copy it saves into its own history.

That information is not sent to United Visions. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Permissions

The direct download asks for Accessibility so it can paste the clip you choose into the app you are using. That permission is used for paste, not to watch what you do in other apps. The Mac App Store version does not request Accessibility. After you select an item, you paste it yourself with ⌘V.

Update checks

If you installed clip'M from the Mac App Store and check for an update, the app asks Apple whether a newer version is listed. That request includes the app’s bundle identifier. It does not include your clipboard.

If you installed the direct download, clip'M uses Sparkle to look for a new release on GitHub. That check does not include your clipboard.

Deleting your information

Clear History removes saved clipboard history from the live database, clears the current clipboard, and retires the key that encrypted that history. It keeps your snippets and actions. Copies in backups, exports, or other apps are not removed. Encryption does not hide saved clips from someone who can use this Mac while it is unlocked. To remove the app's stored data, quit it and delete its ClipM application support folder (inside its app container for the App Store version). Removing the app does not by itself remove that folder.
