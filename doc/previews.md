When using the keyboard shortcut to launch popups on the left of the screen:
* the previews will always appear directly to the right of the pop-up, unless a submenu is opened, and then the preview will open directly to the right of the submenu.

When the pop-up is opened on the right-hand side of the screen:
* the arrows and icons will all be flipped. 
* Submenus will open to the left, and previews will open to the left of the pop-up, unless the submenu is opened, and then the preview will open to the left of the submenu. 

When opening the menu bar pop-up:
* submenus will open to the left, and previews will open to the left, unless a submenu is opened, then the preview will open to the left of the submenu. 

When the arrows and icons are flipped, ensure that keyboard navigation will still work. For example, pushing Q will always jump to quit. 

As the submenus are sometimes opening to the left or the right, we can always open a submenu with the left or right arrow key, that then defines which arrow direction closes the submenu with the left or right arrow key.
